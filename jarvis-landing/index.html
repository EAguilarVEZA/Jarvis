Seven scheduled skills

The systems that make it compound

Each skill reads context, writes an output, and stores memory.

SKILL 01

Morning brief

Daily · 6AM

The one thing, open loops, project status, intelligence and decision flags — under 5 minutes, vault-grounded.

SKILL 02

Capture processor

Daily · 8PM

Routes every raw capture — ideas, tasks, decisions, references, insights — to its home. Nothing stays in the inbox.

SKILL 03

Connection finder

Nightly · 11PM

Links two notes you'd never connect, explains what it reveals, and why it matters. Surprising links beat obvious overlap.

SKILL 04

Weekly synthesis

Sunday · 7PM

Your week in one honest line — what advanced, what stalled, belief updates, the pattern, and next week's single priority.

SKILL 05

Belief tracker

Monday · 8AM

Tests your beliefs against the last 7 days of evidence. Confirmed, challenged, or complicated — honest challenge over confirmation bias.

SKILL 06

Pattern detector

1st of month

Recurrence, convergence, contradiction, emergence and blind spots across months of notes. One real pattern beats ten superficial ones.

SKILL 07

Decision intelligence

Structure + review

Structures new decisions and revisits old ones against today's evidence — continue, pivot, or sunset, and why.
