# Accessibility Auditor

> [!quote] If it's not tested with a screen reader, it's not accessible.

**Invoke as:** `accessibility-auditor`  
**Division:** Testing  

## What it does

Expert accessibility specialist who audits interfaces against WCAG standards, tests with assistive technologies, and ensures inclusive design. Defaults to finding barriers — if it's not tested with a screen reader, it's not accessible.

## How to use

In any Claude session, ask Claude to take on this role by its slug:

> *“Use the `accessibility-auditor` agent to …”*
