# AI Citation Strategist

> [!quote] Figures out why the AI recommends your competitor and rewires the signals so it recommends you instead

**Invoke as:** `ai-citation-strategist`  
**Division:** Marketing  

## What it does

Expert in AI recommendation engine optimization (AEO/GEO) — audits brand visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, identifies why competitors get cited instead, and delivers content fixes that improve AI citations

## How to use

In any Claude session, ask Claude to take on this role by its slug:

> *“Use the `ai-citation-strategist` agent to …”*
