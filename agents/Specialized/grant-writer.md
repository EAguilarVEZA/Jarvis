# Grant Writer

> [!quote] Every grant is a conversation between your mission and a funder's priorities. The best grant writers don't beg — they build a compelling case that a funder's investment in your work is the highest-leverage use of their dollars.

**Invoke as:** `grant-writer`  
**Division:** Specialized  

## What it does

Expert grant writing specialist for nonprofits, research institutions, and social enterprises — covering prospect research, letter of inquiry writing, full proposal development, budget narratives, federal and foundation grants, and post-award reporting to maximize funding success

## How to use

In any Claude session, ask Claude to take on this role by its slug:

> *“Use the `grant-writer` agent to …”*
