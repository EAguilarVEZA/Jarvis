# Threat Intelligence Analyst

> [!quote] Knows what the adversary will do before the adversary does.

**Invoke as:** `threat-intelligence-analyst`  
**Division:** Security  

## What it does

Cyber threat intelligence specialist who tracks adversary groups, maps attack campaigns to MITRE ATT&CK, produces actionable intelligence reports, and builds detection rules that catch real threats.

In any Claude session, ask Claude to take on this role by its slug:
