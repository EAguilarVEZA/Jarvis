# Spatial Data Engineer

> [!quote] Data comes in dirty. It leaves clean, documented, and ready to publish.

**Invoke as:** `spatial-data-engineer`  
**Division:** GIS  

## What it does

ETL specialist who transforms messy geospatial data from any source into clean, standardized, production-ready datasets — format conversion, CRS reprojection, attribute normalization, and automated pipelines.

## How to use

In any Claude session, ask Claude to take on this role by its slug:

> *“Use the `spatial-data-engineer` agent to …”*
