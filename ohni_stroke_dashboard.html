The Stroke landing page ranks as the 9th most visited OHNI page organically, signaling strong intent without paid support.

Strategic Recommendations
🎯 Launch a Dedicated Stroke Campaign
Priority: HIGH · Estimated impact: 150–200 incremental leads/quarter
Current Stroke traffic is entirely organic (#9 page, zero paid support). Adding a dedicated Paid Search campaign targeting high-intent keywords would capture patients at the exact moment they're seeking care:

Recommended keywords: "stroke symptoms," "stroke treatment Orlando," "stroke specialist near me," "TIA doctor," "mini stroke symptoms," "stroke recovery center"

Estimated CPL: $30–$50 based on current Brand campaign efficiency.
Suggested budget: $5,000–$8,000/month.
📈 Fix Facebook Lead Tracking
Priority: HIGH · Zero leads attributed from 405K impressions
Facebook drove 405,202 impressions and 5,303 clicks (1.31% CTR — above the 0.83% benchmark) but shows zero leads. This is a tracking gap, not a performance gap.

Action items:
1. Verify Meta Pixel is firing on the Thank You/Confirmation page
2. Add lead form events to the Facebook conversion API
3. Once tracking is verified, evaluate true CPL for NeuroSpine and expand to Stroke

Estimated hidden leads: 50–80 untracked per month.
💰 Scale January Budget
Priority: MEDIUM · Predictable seasonal opportunity
Jan 11, 2026 delivered 65 leads — the single highest week of the period. Post-holiday neurological care-seeking is a consistent annual pattern.

Recommendation: Increase paid search budget by 25–30% in the first 3 weeks of January. Allocate specifically toward Stroke and Brand campaigns. Pre-plan creative refreshes by December 15.
🌐 Stroke Landing Page Optimization
Priority: MEDIUM · Engagement rate below OHNI average
The Stroke page has 43.75% engagement rate vs 66% for the main OHNI page. Patients are arriving but not engaging enough to convert.

Recommended tests:
1. Add a "Know Your Stroke Risk" symptoms checklist above the fold
2. Feature a patient story or physician video (the /our-team page has 85% engagement — proof that physicians drive trust)
3. Add a prominent "Schedule Now" CTA that opens the appointment form inline
4. Mobile optimization: 54.5% of traffic is mobile
